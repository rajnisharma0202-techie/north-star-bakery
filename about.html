Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to give the neighborhood a welcoming place for dependable, freshly baked food. Our team combines traditional baking methods with seasonal ideas so customers can find familiar favorites as well as something new.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on quality ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Whenever practical, we choose seasonal ingredients and work with suppliers that can provide consistent flour, dairy, fruit, and other baking essentials. Small-batch preparation helps us reduce waste while keeping our daily selection fresh.

Meet the Team

Lead Baker

Our lead baker oversees the bread schedule, develops seasonal loaves, and helps maintain consistent quality from mixing through baking.

Pastry and Cake Team

Our pastry team prepares morning pastries, celebration cakes, and special requests for birthdays, family gatherings, and community events.
