Welcome to North Star Bakery

We are a neighborhood bakery creating fresh breads, pastries, and celebration treats with a warm, handmade touch. Stop in on your commute, bring the family, or contact us about baked goods for your next gathering.

What We're Known For

  • Freshly baked artisan breads
  • Handmade pastries for breakfast and afternoon treats
  • Celebration cakes and special-order baked goods
  • Friendly service for local families, commuters, and events

Featured Item of the Week

Honey Oat Hearth Loaf

Our weekly feature is a hearty loaf with a lightly sweet honey finish and toasted oat topping. It is a great choice for breakfast toast, sandwiches, or sharing at the table.
